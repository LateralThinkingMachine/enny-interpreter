# Enny development roadmap

The open-source preview is a personal meeting interpreter. One listener selects the meeting tab and hears the translation in headphones. Other attendees hear the original meeting. The offline browser sample and local rehearsal are free. For live desktop interpretation, the listener approves an estimate before starting. OpenAI can bill the account that owns the key once a provider connection opens. [Getting started with Enny](docs/get-started-with-enny.md)

## Where Enny is headed

These are plans for meetings where other people also want to hear Enny. The public board shows the current status of each feature. [Follow the Enny roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/LateralThinkingMachine/projects/1/views/1)

| Planned feature | What it would let you do |
| --- | --- |
| [Invite Enny from a Mac](https://github.com/LateralThinkingMachine/enny-roadmap/issues/1) | One organizer would set up Enny once and invite it to a supported meeting. Other attendees would hear the interpretation in their usual meeting app without installing anything. |
| [Check that others can hear Enny before live interpretation](https://github.com/LateralThinkingMachine/enny-roadmap/issues/2) | After attendees agree, the organizer could play a short phrase for free and ask a listener what they heard. Starting paid interpretation would require separate approval. |
| [Run an interpreted meeting from a browser](https://github.com/LateralThinkingMachine/enny-roadmap/issues/3) | An organizer could use a paid managed service without hosting it. It would require an account and a payment method. The organizer would see who pays and the estimate before approving live use. If the confirmation goes missing, they could check the meeting status. |
